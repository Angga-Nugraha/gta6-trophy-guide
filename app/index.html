    Roadmap to the Platinum: Vice Got a Price!

    About 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, say the guides. Three stages, in order. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter: there are 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave, no mission replay and no New Game+.

    Missables & warnings

    Rules for the whole run

    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep cheats off for the whole trophy run; all four guides agree on this.
    • No difficulty settings. There is nothing to set, and no trophy depends on how a mission is played.
    • No mission replay, no New Game+. Free roam continues after the story, and every trophy except possibly one stays doable there.
    • Do not push the Criminal Profile to Irredeemable. It is permanent and makes police far more aggressive.
    • Turn on notifications. Switch on progress, collectible and Criminal Profile notifications in Settings so counters stay visible.
    • Chapter trophies pop late. A chapter trophy pops when the next chapter's opening mission is done, not at the end cutscene. A delayed pop is normal.
    • Non-trophy missables do not block the Platinum. Some optional missions disappear after a while, some quest-givers vanish temporarily and return, and a few magazine collectibles sit in story buildings that later lock but respawn elsewhere. None of them are trophy-relevant.

    All 54 other trophies: not missable

    Story trophies are automatic, and side questlines, robberies, collectibles, activities and challenges all stay available in post-story free roam. Counters are cumulative.

    100% completion: what A Long Road actually requires

    The checklist unlocks after the story at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient, closer to 60% true completion. Progress made during the story already counts.

    Required in full

    • All robberies
    • All 8 bank vaults
    • All 39 gang operations
    • Underwater discoveries
    • Every Shooting Range gold medal (42)
    • All drone races (42 across 14 locations)
    • All 15 of Bret’s Target Decks

    Required in part

    • Any 100 optional missions
    • 200 of 277 base-model vehicle scans
    • 100 of 255 animal studies (binoculars)
    • 15 of 18 meditations
    • 70 of 80 challenges
    • 300 of 397 stunts
    • 100 WAiNK scans

    Not required for 100%

    • Most side quests
    • Strangers & Freaks strands
    • Most collectible sets (only 3 card sets are needed for their own trophy, not for 100%)
